The story

An old game. A new kind of war.

We love chess. We also love card games, where every hand feels new. So we asked a simple question: what happens if you put them together?

Inchoate Checkmate is the answer. The rules of chess stay the same. But on top of the board sits a deck of cards. Each card can trap, shield, swap, boost or destroy pieces. You still need to think like a chess player, but now you also need to adapt like a card player.

It is built in Unity by five friends from Amman, with hand-made 3D pieces, illustrated cards, custom shaders and effects, and a dark-fantasy world all its own.

The opponent

An AI that taught itself to play

A normal chess engine breaks when cards change the rules every turn. So we did not write one. We trained our AI through self-play: it played against itself again and again, and slowly learned which cards to play and when.

At first it played badly. Then it got better. The day it beat its own programmer for the first time, we knew it was real.

Play itself→ Win or lose→ Learn→ Play again

With your support we can train it longer and give it different difficulty levels, from friendly to ruthless.

FAQ

Who is running this campaign?

Echo-V is a five-person indie studio in Amman, Jordan. Kickstarter does not support creators in Jordan yet, so the campaign is launched by our family member in the United States, who manages the account and payments for the team. The game, the team and all the work are in Jordan.

What platform is the game for?

PC (Windows) on Steam. We may look at other platforms later, but we will only promise PC.

Do I need to know chess?

It helps, but no. If you know how the pieces move, you can play. The cards teach you the rest.

Is there multiplayer?

Local play and the AI opponent are the base plan. Online play is a stretch goal.

When do digital rewards arrive?

Steam keys, art book and credits come at launch. Beta access comes before launch. All rewards are digital, so there is no shipping cost anywhere in the world.

Can I change my pledge?

Yes, any time before the campaign ends, on Kickstarter.
